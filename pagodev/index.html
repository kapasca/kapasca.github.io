FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you build custom features?

Custom development is our specialty. We build software tailored to your workflows, with custom features, automation, and AI integration.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different. Our services start from IDR 2 million, depending on features, complexity, and development scope.

How long does development take?

Small projects may take around one week, while larger systems can require several months depending on their complexity.

Do you provide warranty?

Yes. We provide post-launch support, bug fixes, and feature warranty to ensure your application runs reliably.

Do you serve clients outside your city?

Yes. We work remotely with clients across Indonesia and internationally. As long as communication runs smoothly, distance is never a limitation.
